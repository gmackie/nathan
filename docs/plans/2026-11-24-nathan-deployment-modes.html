Nathan deployment modes In review

November 24, 2026 · Nathan · Cloudflare Workers and D1

Overview

Standalone issuer

One identity Worker and identity database serve many registered OIDC clients. Each application has its own callback, client ID and session.

Built-in auth

An app mounts the same Nathan runtime inside its Worker on its own HTTPS origin. Its identity database, provider settings, host-only sessions and Kumo Studio belong to that installation.

Integration contract

Standalone apps discover the issuer at /api/auth/.well-known/openid-configuration. An app that owns its identity data uses withNathan to mount Better Auth and Studio. It keeps its home page, health endpoint and business routes. A peer Worker can pass a Request to NathanService.handle for protocol and management calls in either mode. A peer that only reads one app’s access decisions can bind NathanVerifier to that app.

Trust and operations

Who owns identity data and configuration?

The app that embeds Nathan is trusted with its identity D1 and secrets. Apps connecting over OIDC keep independent sessions and client credentials. Service-binding callers supply the credentials each operation requires. Studio requires a verified administrator and the installation’s configured assurance policy.
