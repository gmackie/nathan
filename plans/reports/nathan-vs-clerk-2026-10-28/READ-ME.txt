Nathan vs Clerk: cost and ownership report
Pricing snapshot: 2026-10-28 | Currency: USD, before tax

Open report.html for the full report and interactive workload calculator.
Open report-static.html for the script-free, expanded, printable edition.
Both reports work without a server or remote assets.

Figures: cost-at-scale.png/svg and one-million-breakdown.png/svg.
CRUD figures: crud-total-cost-at-scale.png/svg and crud-cost-composition.png/svg.
Data: model-inputs.json, model-results.json, baseline-costs.csv,
and sensitivity-costs.csv; crud-total-costs.csv and crud-sensitivity-costs.csv.
Evidence: source-ledger.json and 12 official page snapshots under sources/.
SHA256SUMS records the integrity of every other file in this bundle.

Reproduce the calculations from this directory:
  python3 cost_model.py
  node verify_model.mjs
  uv run --with matplotlib==3.10.7 python make_charts.py
  python3 build_report.py

Python arithmetic uses only the standard library and runs offline. The chart
step requires matplotlib; uv may download it on the first run. The saved charts
and reports need no dependencies. Updating official source snapshots with
fetch_sources.py requires internet and a deliberate review of model-inputs.json;
fetching pages does not automatically change the model's prices.

Prices and vendor limits are sourced facts. Nathan requests, CPU, rows, storage,
mail and labor are explicit assumptions, not measured production usage. Costs
through 1M-5M users are projections and do not establish supported capacity.
They add no 1M-user load-test requirement; the launch population stays 51,000.

Identity-only results exclude application hosting. CRUD results include the same
Workers/D1 app on both sides, with one set of Cloudflare account inclusions and
the $5 base applied once. Authentication's share uses an app-first incremental
allocation: combined app/Nathan bill minus app-only bill, plus auth email.
The original identity-only gateway scenarios are not added to CRUD traffic.
CRUD presets: 100, 1000 or 10000 API requests per active user/month, a write-heavy
variant, and full diagnostic logging. Activity and MRU can vary independently.
All values are illustrative rather than a measured typical-usage benchmark.

Savings exclude development and incremental operation/security work until entered;
unspecified external services need separate budgets. Frequent token refreshes can
make Nathan cost more than Clerk. The modeled 12 GB at 1M stored accounts exceeds
one D1 database's 10 GB limit and needs an unimplemented partitioning design.
The CRUD app adds 100 GB at 1M stored accounts, needing at least ten databases
in either stack before headroom. Low row prices do not establish throughput.
Read the report's assumptions, adverse cases and ownership section with its
headline figures. Recheck vendor prices before public use.
